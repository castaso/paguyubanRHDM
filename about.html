About Paguyuban RHDM

A family site with a market in the back garden

It started in 2019 as a page for reunion photos and grew a market after one summer when three separate people tried to give away courgettes through a group chat. This page explains how the whole thing is meant to work.

Setting up and posting listings is done by the family admins. If you want something on the market, send it to them.

Three steps, and then you talk to a relative

1 · Find it

Browse the market or search by item, person, or place. Every listing says who has it and where.

2 · Message the person

Open a listing and message the seller. There is no cart and no checkout, you are talking to your cousin, not a shop.

3 · Sort it out between you

Agree a price, a pickup, and a time. Cash or a favour, at the gate or at the reunion, whatever the two of you decide.

Four things everyone agreed to

  1. Be kind. It is a market between relatives. A grudge costs more than the table.
  2. Describe it honestly. The water ring, the missing lid, the wobble. Say it in the listing, not at the handover.
  3. Answer within a week. If you cannot, put that in the description so nobody is left waiting.
  4. Keep it in the family. No buying to resell outside. This is not a business.

Anything broken, missing, or wrong on the site goes to the organiser. Anything wrong with a listing goes to the person who posted it.

Posting and managing listings is limited to the admins.
